**SquadLab for NLM — Beta Testers Wanted**

I'm opening SquadLab up to a small group of beta testers.

SquadLab is a free Windows companion app for NLM that helps you use the attributes shown at **Your Level** to:

• rank your squad by position  
• build your Best XI + bench  
• see squad weaknesses / recruitment priorities  
• compare formations with **Tactic Effectiveness**  
• add scouted players and see if they improve your squad  
• sign players / move players on  
• manage saves and seasons  

Everything is stored locally on your PC.

**Current beta:** v3.4.0  
**Platform:** Windows PC

⚠️ The beta installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show an **Unknown publisher** warning. If you've downloaded it from my official SquadLab release page, use **More info → Run anyway**.
