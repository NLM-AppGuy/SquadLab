# SquadLab for NLM

**SquadLab for NLM** is a free Windows companion app for **Non League Manager**, created by **NLM App Guy**.

It helps you turn the player attributes NLM shows at **Your Level** into a clearer view of your squad: who fits each position best, which formation gets the strongest XI from the players you have, where the weak areas are, and whether a scouted player actually improves you.

> SquadLab is an independent fan-made companion and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the developers of Non League Manager.

## What SquadLab does

- Rank players by position using the attributes NLM shows at **Your Level**
- Build a **Best XI** for your selected formation
- Recommend a balanced **subs bench**
- Show **squad health** and recruitment priorities
- Compare every supported formation in **Tactic Effectiveness**
- Add **scouted players** and compare them with your current squad
- Move signed scouts directly into your squad
- Identify players who may be outside your XI, bench and key cover
- Manage multiple saves / clubs and start new seasons
- Back up and restore your SquadLab data

SquadLab does **not** attempt to reverse-engineer hidden NLM player ability. It works only from the information the game shows you.

## Your data

SquadLab is **local-first**.

Your squads, scouted players and saves are stored locally on your PC. SquadLab does not require an account or upload your squad database to me.

Updates are designed to replace the app while retaining your existing local data.

I still recommend using **Backup & Import** periodically, especially before changing PC or clearing browser/app data.

## How scoring works

NLM displays attributes such as **Excellent**, **Very Good**, **Good**, **Average** and so on at **Your Level**.

SquadLab uses those visible attributes to create a numerical positional rating. Different positions value different attributes, so the same player can be a stronger fit in one position than another.

The exact weighting model is part of SquadLab's internal logic, but the important point is simple:

**SquadLab compares players using the information NLM gives you at your current level.**

The score is a squad-management aid, not a prediction of match performance.
